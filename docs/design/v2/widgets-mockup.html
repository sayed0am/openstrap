Widgets in the new design

The five home screen widgets moved onto the app's picture cards. Each section puts today's widget (left, on the grey home screen) beside the new one at Small 2x2, Wide 4x2 and Large 4x3. Sample data is the widgets' own preview data.

  • No more edge wash. Recovery, Steps, Sleep and Start are dark picture cards, like their cards in the app. Usual days stay dark.
  • Each widget draws its card's picture in its own colour: Recovery a week of dots, Steps the hours as pill bars, Sleep the night's stages. Numbers are white dots, status lines grey.
  • News is a tag, off is a rim. Goal met and the goal ladder as a tag after the number. A night or a score off your usual gets the rim next to the part of the picture that is off, and its tag.
  • Moment keeps its glow. It is the widget form of Today's hero, which is still a glow card in the app. Only its colours follow the new moments (after a run in Recovery teal).
  • Kept from the widget rules: system sans for text, the three sizes, the amber round strap button when the strap needs you, no wrapped captions.

Recovery

The app's Recovery card: the week as dots in teal, today larger, the score in dots under it. Wide puts the dots beside the number, and Large adds the sentence and the three figures. A low score lights the rim beside today's dot with Below usual.

Steps

The hours as lime pill bars, the latest hour full, as on Today's Steps card. Wide adds the goal as the note and how many to go, and Large adds Your week as rings. Past the goal, the ladder's tag after the number.

Sleep

The night's stages: one bar on the Small, the four lanes on Wide and Large like the Last night card. A short night lights the rim at the end of the night with Short.

Start a workout

Unlit like the app's Start card, which has a light travelling round its border. A widget can't move, so it keeps a still red light on one corner. Tiles as in the app.

Moment

Today's hero on the home screen, a glow that follows the moment. Midday, workout time and evening shown. The strap button here shows a stale sync.
