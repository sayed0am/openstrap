Design system

One set of rules for light, colour, type, shape, lines and motion, and for how each moment of the day changes them. Every screen gets checked against this page. Where it disagrees with an older doc, mockup or the code, this page wins, and spec.md is rewritten to match.

Light levels

Light is the loudest thing in the app, so it comes in fixed levels. A glow lights the hero and at most one more card. Every other card stays dark until its value leaves your normal, and then a rim lights next to the part of its picture that is off. Nothing glows only to decorate.

  • One hero, one second. At most one L4 and one L3 glow on a screen. Every other card is L2 or L0.
  • Off your normal is light and a word, never a new colour. A card goes from L0 to L2: the rim lights where its picture is off, and a tag says what. The number stays white. The hairline, the tier and the hue never change.
  • No light on a usual card, a list or a strip. The soft edge glow on every card (L1) is gone: when every card was lit, the light said nothing and every card looked alike.
  • Text sits in the dark. White text keeps at least 4.5:1 against what is behind it. A glow's body colour may reach a luminance of 0.18.
  • Locked on every glow: 28 dp corners, the grain, the hairline, the black the core fades to, text top left. Free per card: hue, where the light comes from, how high it reaches.

02

Colour

Each metric owns one ink colour, and it says which metric a card is about, never how it is going. The picture is always in it, the number never is. The glow tones are worked out from the ink by one formula, so every card of a metric looks like the same light. Ramps below: ink, then light, body and core.

  • Colour says which metric. Pictures and chart marks are always in the ink, usual or not. Dotted numbers and row values are always white, status lines are Secondary. News is a tag (06).
  • On a glow card the glow is the colour, so its marks stay white.
  • Light is the ink's hue at 90% of its saturation and 56% lightness. Body is 80% saturation, 16% lightness. Core is 70% saturation, 3% lightness.
  • Energy becomes Ember orange #F66C28 on every surface. Gold #FFD166 and a red to green scale
  • Recovery keeps one teal. readiness and readinessDeep
  • Evening violet lights only the evening hero. It is a moment, not a metric.
  • Green and amber are for the strap and sync only, never for a health value.
  • No neutral light. Strips, Your week and Workouts are unlit. Your week draws its rings in Steps' lime.

Half size. On a 390 dp phone a unit is 173 by 160 dp. A Tall is two units and the gap. A lone Small becomes a Wide. The hero sizes to its content with no empty band under the last line.

A card's size follows how much it has to show

  • Scored on five things. Four about what its data has, 0 to 2 each: a shape over the day or night, a normal to compare with, a history, parts that make it up. One about how much it changes your day, 0 to 3.
  • On its tab a card takes the size its score earns. The most detailed lead their tab, single values become rows.
  • Today is a grid one size down. Each card shows one size smaller than on its tab, so Smalls pair up: Steps a Wide, Heart rate, Load and Stress Smalls. A pair never follows a pair.
  • Order on every page: the subject, what explains it (a list), cards by weight with Smalls in pairs, then things about the last weeks.

  • Each metric draws its own picture, and neighbours never share one. A card's design is its tier, its picture and its shape. Colour alone does not make two cards different.
  • Never two neighbours alike. Two Wides never touch on a tab, a list sits between them. A pair never follows a pair on Today.
  • Tappable says so. A card that opens something shows a 14 dp caret right after its title, at 50% white. A row ends with the same caret at 30%. A card or row that opens nothing shows none. Pressed, both scale to 0.98.
  • (i) lives in the card. A 32 dp round button at 10% white, top right of the title row. It opens the info sheet. No "How it works" panel, no separate card.
  • Number and word. The word after the digits ("bpm", "asleep", "today") sits on their baseline in Body 15 at 72%.
  • Pages under a card (Habit Age, every detail) use the same tiers. DotPanel and DotHeader are gone everywhere.

Motion

Motion tells you where something came from, what changed, or that it is alive. Springs never bounce. Nothing moves when idle except the live heart rate.

Frame

The header and the tab bar are the same on every tab. Pages opened over a tab share one header of their own.

  • Header: avatar (44 dp) left, the moment or tab name over the date in the middle, the strap chip right. The chip shows words only when they matter: "Charging 64%", "Not synced", "18 min ago".
  • Tab bar: four tabs in a centred pill, the active one tinted with its section colour. A black fade 120 dp tall sits behind it, so cards slide under it softly instead of cutting into its edge. The last card ends 24 dp above the pill.
  • Pages (You, Strap, details, Habit Age, Alarms, Firmware): a 44 dp round back button, the page title in Display 24, then cards in the same tiers. No Material panels or list items.
  • Sheets: the Sheet surface, 28 dp top corners, a 36 by 4 dp handle, the same type roles.
13

Unfolded

On the inner screen of the Fold, and anything 600 dp wide or more, the same rules hold in two columns.

  • Two columns with a 12 dp gap and a 24 dp gutter. Content stops at 960 dp and centres.
  • Type and dot sizes stay the same. Cards do not grow taller, they only get wider.
  • The hero and strips span both columns. A Pair or a Tall with its Smalls stays together in one column.
  • Neighbours include cards side by side, so the no two alike rule checks across columns too.
  • Pages opened over a tab (details, You, Strap) take one centred column, 600 dp at most.

  • No arrows, triangles or dotted arrows. An arrow says up but not whether up is good: resting heart rate up is bad news, HRV up is good news.
  • No red and green for change. Colour only says which metric. Off your normal is the rim and a tag, never a judgement.
