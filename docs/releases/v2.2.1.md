**Home screen widgets work again.** In 2.2.0, widgets added to the home screen stayed on their loading placeholder and never showed your figures. 2.2.1 fixes that.

## Fixes

- Home screen widgets load and refresh again. The release build had stripped a part the widgets need to draw, so they never left the loading placeholder.

## Changes

- Updated the background job library the widgets run on, and the app's other libraries, to their current versions.

## Upgrading

Installs over 2.2.0 and keeps all your data and settings. Android 9 or newer. Widgets you already placed fill in on their own a few seconds after the update.
