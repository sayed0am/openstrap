**OpenStrap is now unstrap.** Same app, same data, a new name. 3.0 is also the biggest update yet: workouts rebuilt from start to summary, sleep and naps you can fix yourself, goals and bests, cards that show their own picture, and Android 11 support.

## Workouts, rebuilt

- **Start a workout** is one screen with every sport, your usual one first with Start, a search and your recent ones. Only sports the strap records are offered.
- **Ready** fills itself from last time: goal, map, heart rate and the strap's buzz, with a layout for each kind of sport.
- **A live screen for each kind.** Runs and rides with pace, splits and the map. Indoor cardio. Strength with sets, rest and an optional log. Intervals with HIIT presets, run reps, saved workouts and named buzzes. Yoga and mind and body with a calm clock and heart line, its dimmed screen still showing the time. Games.
- **On the lock screen.** The live workout shows over the lock screen, and its controls sit beside it on a phone turned on its side. Every live screen fits a half-folded Fold.
- **Heart rate from the strap's own workout session**, one live heart rate for every screen. The Live heart rate switch is gone.
- **A summary for each kind of workout**, with what you earned, Share and Done. Past workouts open as summaries too, and one trim bar trims a workout, a sleep or a nap.
- **Share pictures** for every kind of workout and for your week, all in one layout, with a photo behind if you like.
- **Weekly goal, bests and badges**, with a celebration when you reach them.
- **Heart rate zones**: one set for every sport, on a page of its own with your max heart rate, on one cool to hot colour scale. A workout's top card takes the colour of the zone you were mostly in.
- **Call buzz length**: the strap buzzes for 5, 10 or 15 seconds, or the whole ring.

## Sleep and naps

- Adjust when you fell asleep and woke up, for the night and for naps.
- Delete a night or a nap, with Undo. Deleted sleep keeps them under You so you can restore them, and a deleted night says so wherever it would show.
- After a big change to a night, the strap's sleep score is adjusted rather than replaced.
- A nap whose heart rate stayed as it does when sitting still says it might not be a nap.
- When the strap's own sleep result is missing or covers the wrong hours, unstrap works out the night from the strap's minute by minute signal. These nights have no stages, the Sleep card says the strap missed the night, and Energy still recharges. This is under testing.

## The new look

- **Cards with their own picture.** Each card on Today, Recovery, Sleep and Activity draws what it measures: a line, a level, a count or a ring. A coloured rim shows when it's off your normal, and a tag says how ("Double goal", "Above normal"). Today is laid out as a grid.
- **Widgets in the new design**: all five are dark picture cards, matching the app.
- **The strap's notification tells you your day**: the moment and its number ("Evening, Energy 35") over Today's sentence, with the strap's battery and last sync in its header.
- Start a workout's moving border is a chroma ring, and Search all sports and More sports slide in instead of jumping.

## Closer to the research

- Sleep debt, sleep headlines and three Healthspan terms read more clearly.
- Walking effort, short heart rate runs, body signal and stress windows, run VO2max and long runs follow the studies more closely.
- Night signals compare the night with the nights before it, the same on every tab, and say how far their normal has moved. Night blood oxygen comes from the all-day readings.
- The recovery index shows in Readiness but no longer counts toward the score.

## Other changes

- Android 11 support: unstrap now installs on Android 11 and newer.
- Before Android 14, where Health Connect is an app of its own, unstrap offers to install it when it's missing.
- A Keep recording notice when Android won't let a workout run in the background.
- Save a copy and Restore open in Downloads.
- A faster app, from the first launch after an install or update.
- Strap firmware updates are off for now while I look into a sleep bug in firmware 3.27.0.1.

## Fixes

- No lost activity minutes and no doubled workouts after a sync, and a sync that fails or times out ends cleanly.
- Settings and backups can no longer lose data, and a restore fills in every row the phone is missing, on any day.
- After pairing the strap again, the app fetches what the strap still holds.
- Health Connect gets whole hours of heart rate, and an import no longer adds a second night.
- Today, the widgets and the week bars follow the day and every edit.
- Cards no longer flash their flat colour when they resize, and the route map hides when a run went nowhere.
- Zepp sign-in works again after Zepp changed its login page.
- The skin temperature card says how far you are from your normal once, not twice.
- Long sport names wrap at their hyphen, and tags under a dotted number no longer crowd it.

## Upgrading

Installs over 2.2.2 and keeps all your data, settings, Health Connect records and backups. Backups made before 3.0 are still found and restored. Android 11 or newer.
