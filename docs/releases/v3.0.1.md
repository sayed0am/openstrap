A fix for sleep worked out from the strap's minute by minute signal.

## Fixes

- **A right night is no longer replaced.** When the app caught up several days of minutes at once, after a strap restart such as a firmware update, the minutes could come back hours late. 3.0.0 then took the strap's own night for wrong and replaced it with one worked out from those late minutes, which could land in the daytime. Now a night the strap sent with its stages stays, unless it's a small part of the night the minutes found.
- **No night is worked out from minutes caught up after more than a day.** Those nights keep the strap's own result.
- **Nights replaced by mistake come back.** After the update, unstrap fetches the strap's sleep results for the last three weeks again (the strap keeps about 60 days), so a night 3.0.0 replaced shows the strap's own version once more.

## Upgrading

Installs over 3.0.0 and keeps all your data. Android 11 or newer.
